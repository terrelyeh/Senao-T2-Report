TICKET #100192918
Regarding LBD Function Behavior
Affected: KOKOMO-S210MGT-P Opened: May 12, 2026 Last Response: May 25, 2026 Closed: May 25, 2026
Resolved
Case Description

We have been testing the LBD (Loop Detection) function via the local web interface. During our verification, we observed the following behavior when STP is disabled:

  • No logs are generated when a loop is detected
  • No blocked port is displayed
  • The switch goes offline

We understand that this configuration may involve unsupported functionality. However, we would like to confirm:

  • Is enabling STP required in order to properly use the LBD function?
  • Is this behavior expected when STP is disabled?
Case Summary

Issue: Apresia reported that the Loop Detection (LBD) feature appeared not to function correctly when STP was disabled. In the Cloud Portal, loop events did not generate logs or show blocked ports, and the switch temporarily went offline.

Investigation Findings:

  • LBD and STP are independent features; LBD does not require STP to operate.
  • Local GUI testing confirmed that LBD correctly detected loops, generated logs, and blocked the affected port.
  • The apparent lack of logs in the Cloud Portal was primarily due to the switch becoming temporarily unreachable during the loop event and only synchronizing information after connectivity was restored.
  • Further testing identified that LBD must be enabled on all participating ports, including the uplink port, for loop detection to function as expected when ports belong to the same VLAN.
  • When downlink ports were placed in a different VLAN from the uplink port, LBD operated correctly even without enabling LBD on the uplink port because loop traffic was contained within the isolated VLAN.

Conclusion: The observed behavior was determined to be expected behavior, not a defect. LBD functionality depends on the VLAN topology and port-group participation. For loops occurring within the same VLAN domain, the uplink port must also be included in LBD protection. The customer confirmed the explanation and closed the case.

TICKET #99938446
Question About Communication to s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com
Opened: May 11, 2026 Last Response: May 25, 2026 Closed: May 25, 2026
Resolved
Case Description

Could you please provide information about s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com listed in the firewall rules?

  • What is the purpose of communication to s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com?
  • If the splash page is not used, is it still necessary to allow access to this FQDN?
  • If this communication is not allowed, will it have any impact other than on the splash page feature?
Case Summary

A customer raised concerns regarding outbound communications from KOKOMO Access Points to AWS-related domains, specifically questioning the purpose of connections to s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com, whether they are required when Splash Page functionality is not used, and whether additional observed destinations such as aws.amazon.com and CloudFront endpoints should be permitted.

The investigation confirmed that s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com is used to host Splash Page and Captive Portal assets stored in AWS S3. While these communications are primarily associated with Splash Page functionality, blocking access could potentially affect cloud-delivered resources and future feature compatibility.

Further analysis of customer proxy logs showed periodic connections to aws.amazon.com every approximately 180 seconds, followed by HTTPS sessions to AWS CloudFront endpoints and communications with KOKOMO cloud services. Engineering confirmed that the device performs a connectivity check to aws.amazon.com, and that the customer's understanding of the CloudFront-to-S3 content delivery architecture was correct.

During follow-up discussions, the customer requested clarification on whether additional domains not listed in the original firewall guide needed to be allowed. Engineering confirmed that, in addition to the documented KOKOMO cloud domains and s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com, access to aws.amazon.com and *.cloudfront.net should also be permitted to ensure normal cloud operation and AWS resource accessibility.

Conclusion: The observed AWS and CloudFront communications are expected behavior. Customers implementing restrictive outbound firewall policies should allow the documented KOKOMO cloud domains, s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com, aws.amazon.com, and *.cloudfront.net to avoid potential service disruptions. The customer was informed of the findings and the case was subsequently closed.

TICKET #104130912
Request for Specific FQDNs for "dolphin-jp-*.production.kokomocloud.com"
Opened: May 27, 2026 Last Response: May 27, 2026 Closed: May 27, 2026
Resolved
Case Description

We received an inquiry from a customer who is trying to register firewall rules using a FortiGate 90G.

They reported that they cannot register the policy for dolphin-jp-*.production.kokomocloud.com because their firewall does not accept wildcard domains for this configuration.

To address this, we would like to provide the customer with the specific FQDNs they need to allow. Could you please provide us with the detailed list of specific FQDNs included under dolphin-jp-*.production.kokomocloud.com?

Case Summary

A customer using a FortiGate 90G firewall reported that they could not create a firewall policy using the wildcard domain dolphin-jp-*.production.kokomocloud.com, as their firewall platform does not support wildcard FQDN entries for this use case.

The customer requested the specific FQDNs covered by the wildcard entry. Engineering confirmed that the wildcard currently resolves to the following two hostnames:

  • dolphin-jp-001.production.kokomocloud.com
  • dolphin-jp-002.production.kokomocloud.com

The customer also requested confirmation that allowing these two FQDNs individually would provide the same functionality as allowing the wildcard domain. Engineering confirmed that permitting both hostnames is functionally equivalent to permitting the wildcard entry.

Conclusion: The customer was advised to create firewall policies for dolphin-jp-001.production.kokomocloud.com and dolphin-jp-002.production.kokomocloud.com individually. This provides the same operational behavior as allowing the wildcard domain. The customer acknowledged the guidance and the case was closed successfully.

TICKET #100945961
Planned Additions of Captive Portal Authentication Methods
Opened: May 15, 2026 Last Response: Jun 10, 2026
Open
Case Description

We have received an inquiry from a customer regarding the planned addition of authentication types for our captive portal. Do you have any plans to add authentication types such as the following?

  • Social Media Authentication
  • Email Authentication
  • SMS Authentication
Case Summary

A customer inquired whether KOKOMO Cloud has plans to support additional Captive Portal authentication methods, including Social Media Authentication, Email Authentication, and SMS Authentication.

Initial investigation confirmed that these authentication methods are not currently available as standard KOKOMO Cloud features, and there are no plans to add Social Media or SMS authentication. Customers requiring these capabilities are expected to leverage third-party captive portal providers and regional service integrations.

The discussion then focused on potential integration with Social WiFi, a third-party WiFi marketing platform. Testing revealed that Social WiFi could not recognize certain EnGenius and KOKOMO access points due to OUI (MAC vendor prefix) validation restrictions within Social WiFi's device whitelist database. Specifically, KOKOMO APs were rejected because their OUI was not registered as a supported vendor, while some EnGenius models were limited to specific approved OUIs.

Further investigation determined that the issue was not caused by KOKOMO Cloud itself but by Social WiFi's vendor validation mechanism. A ticket was raised with Social WiFi, which subsequently disabled MAC verification for the test environment and confirmed that supported EnGenius OUIs could be added successfully. Additional testing with newer supported EnGenius devices verified that captive portal integration and login functionality worked as expected.

During the investigation, Product Management roadmap information confirmed that Email Authentication for Captive Portal is planned for Q3, while Social Media and SMS authentication remain unsupported and are not currently on the product roadmap.
